# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

The full text is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

In short: be respectful and constructive. Harassment and discriminatory behaviour are not tolerated,
in any project space or in public spaces when representing the project.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer at **justin.eiletz@jexcellence.de**. All
reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
